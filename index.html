What's a WhiteSpace?

A WhiteSpace is a virtual world for you and your friends to roleplay in! It's got proximity chat, and works across any device with a browser connection (I think. It's supposed to, anyway. I'm still working on it.) It's self-hosted, meaning one of you runs the server on your computer. You can upload & use your own custom images & 3D models, or just use your imagination!
