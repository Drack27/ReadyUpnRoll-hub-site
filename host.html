How to host, step by step

  1. Download the host app

    Click the big button above. The file lands in your Downloads folder.

  2. Double-click it to start

    Open your Downloads folder and double-click the file.

    ⚠️ Windows might show a blue box saying "Windows protected your PC." That's normal for small, independent apps. Click More info, then Run anyway.

  3. Allow it through the firewall

    If Windows asks whether to allow the app on your network, click Allow. This lets your players connect.

  4. Your game opens in your browser

    Give it a few seconds. A browser tab opens with your room, ready to go.

  5. Invite your players

    Click Invite in the game. Copy the link and send it to your players by text, Discord, email, anything. Phone players can scan the QR code instead.

  6. Keep the app window open while you play

    A small black window stays open in the background. That's your game server. Closing it ends the game for everyone. When the session's over, close it.

Questions

Do my players need to download anything?
No. Only the host downloads the app. Players just open the invite link in a browser, on a computer or a phone.
Does it cost anything?
No.
Can I host from a Mac or a phone?
Not yet. Hosting needs a Windows computer for now. Anyone can join from any device.
My players' link stopped working.
Each time you start the app, you get a new invite link. Send your players the new one.
Does my computer need to stay on?
Yes, for as long as you're playing. If it sleeps or shuts down, the game ends.
